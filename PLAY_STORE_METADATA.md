## 3. Full Description (Google Play Console Ready)

```text
Are background apps silently draining your daily 1.5 GB or 2 GB mobile data pack? Are unexpected background downloads causing high ping spikes while gaming, or distracting social media notifications interrupting your study and work?

AppData Blocker is an on-device, no-root internet firewall that gives you complete control over which apps can access the internet. Stop background data hogging, save your mobile data pack till midnight, cut down distractions, and protect your privacy—all with one tap.

🛡️ DUAL-SHIELD FIREWALL PROTECTION
• Installed Apps Shield: Block internet for downloaded apps, games, and streaming apps while keeping only your chosen apps online.
• System Apps Shield: Prevent non-essential pre-installed bloatware and background analytics from wasting your bandwidth.
• Essential Core Apps Safeguard: Google Play Services, Android System UI, and core network modules remain safely whitelisted so your phone runs smoothly.

⚡ PERFECT FOR DAILY DATA SAVERS & INDIAN PREPAID USERS
• Running on daily 1GB, 1.5GB, or 2GB daily recharge plans (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL)?
• Stop apps like Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and games from consuming your daily quota in the background.
• Only allow the apps you are actively using right now!

🎮 ZERO PING SPIKES FOR MOBILE GAMERS
• Love playing online multiplayer games (BGMI, Free Fire, Call of Duty)?
• Block data for all other apps so 100% of your network bandwidth is dedicated to your game.
• Say goodbye to sudden lag, ping spikes, and background download stalls.

📚 FOCUS & DISTRACTION-FREE STUDY
• Studying for competitive exams (UPSC, JEE, NEET, SSC) or working on deep tasks?
• Block data to social media and chat apps without turning off your entire Wi-Fi or mobile data. Keep study portals, research tools, or work messaging active without tempting distractions.

🔋 PROLONG BATTERY LIFE
• Apps blocked from background internet access cannot wake up CPU radios or ping servers in loops.
• Enjoy longer battery backup throughout the day.

🔒 100% PRIVACY — NO ROOT & ON-DEVICE
• No root access required: 100% safe for banking and UPI apps.
• Zero remote servers: Your internet traffic is filtered locally on your device using Android's native VpnService loopback.
• No data collection: None of your data, IP packets, or browsing habits are ever uploaded, inspected, or shared.

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• One-Tap Firewall Toggle: Instantly start or stop data blocking.
• Quick App Exemption: Whitelist up to 11 priority apps (e.g., UPI payment apps, important messaging) with a single tap.
• Live Network Speedometer: Monitor real-time upload and download speeds on your dashboard.
• Lightweight & Clean: Modern Material 3 interface, battery-optimized, and free of unnecessary clutter.

---------------------------------------------------
VPN SERVICE TRANSPARENT DISCLOSURE:
AppData Blocker utilizes Android's native VpnService to create a local on-device network loopback interface. This enables the app to block or permit network connections on a per-app basis directly on your phone without requiring root privileges. AppData Blocker does NOT route your internet traffic through any external VPN servers and does NOT collect, decrypt, or log any personal data or browsing activity.
```
